Data connections

Switch from demo data to Google Sheets and later Field Nation

K
The application is running with demo records by default. You do not have to rebuild the dashboard when you connect the real Sheet; you only change environment variables.

1 · Google Sheets pilot

For non-sensitive test data, publish a dedicated reporting tab as CSV and set:

DATA_SOURCE=public_csv SHEET_CSV_URL=https://docs.google.com/.../pub?output=csv

Do not use this method for confidential operational data.

2 · Private Google Sheets connection

Create a Google Cloud service account, enable Sheets API, share the reporting Sheet to the service-account email as Viewer, then set:

DATA_SOURCE=google_service_account GOOGLE_SHEET_ID=... GOOGLE_SHEET_RANGE=Dashboard_Data!A:Z GOOGLE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON={...}

3 · Recommended reporting columns

The connector accepts aliases, but the clean schema is:

site_id, site_name, city, state, region, coordinator, status, request_date, sourcing_started, technician_sourced, scheduled_date, completed_date, technician, platform, labor_cost, travel_cost, materials_cost, hours, revisit, issue, notes

4 · Field Nation phase

The backend was deliberately separated from the GUI. Add a Field Nation service in src/, normalize its work orders to the same fields, and either merge those records with Sheets or use Field Nation as the authoritative source for technician/pay data.

Keep OAuth credentials in Render environment variables, never browser JavaScript.
